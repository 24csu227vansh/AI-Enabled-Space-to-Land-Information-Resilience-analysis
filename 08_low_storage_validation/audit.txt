Because targeted gains are 0.18*w_i*(1-q_i), while balanced gain is their average, the Rule-Based action selecting max weighted deficit is the exact greedy maximizer of the environment performance metric.
Next-state quality is sampled independently of the action; therefore no multi-step action effect exists in this environment. This benchmark cannot substantiate that RL learns improved long-horizon resilience.
